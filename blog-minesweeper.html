Did you know you can play Minesweeper on my website?

So... there is Minesweeper on my website now. I was working on the personal side of the site and thought it would be fun to have something you could actually sit down and play. Completely necessary addition to a cybersecurity portfolio, obviously. You can play it here. There are sound effects too, so clicking a mine gets a proper reaction.

If you've only looked through my projects or resume, there's a bit more tucked away on my About page. I know most people probably aren't visiting my portfolio for Minesweeper, but you're already here. Have a look around, put some music on, and play a round. The resume will still be there when you're done :)
